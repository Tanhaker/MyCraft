What parents look for before they call

Most classes in Rajkot are found on Google and judged in thirty seconds. These are the pages that decide it.

Results

Toppers, percentages, selections — with photos and years. The first thing a parent looks for, and the thing almost no class in Rajkot has online.

Batches & timings

Standard-wise batches, subjects, and timings on one page. Cuts the repeated "kaya batch chalu chhe?" phone calls to your staff.

Fees, stated plainly

Clear fee structure with what's included. Parents trust a class that publishes its fees — and it stops any confusion before admission.

Admission enquiry

A short form that lands directly on your WhatsApp with the student's name, standard and number. No missed calls during class hours.
